Waukesha Condominium Consortium

This information is brought to you by the Waukesha Condominium Consortium. The Consortium is a number of condominium associations who have banded together for the betterment of its members in terms of a relationship with the City of Waukesha, and for leverage from the sharing of mutual practices.

The Waukesha Condominium Consortium is not a legal entity, and is in no way offering legal advice. Prospective owners should thoroughly review the governing documents of the Association of interest, and consult with their realtor or an attorney before making a purchase decision.

Purpose of This Guide

Having a good understanding of what to expect with a condominium purchase is tantamount to an enjoyable living experience.

What Is Different About Condominium Developments?

Condominium developments follow Planned Unit Developments (PUDs) with different street, sidewalk, and setback requirements than single-family homes.

Why Do Condominiums Have an Association?

Associations govern and manage the shared assets and responsibilities of condominium developments.

Responsibilities of the Association

The association maintains and manages all limited common elements including grounds, exteriors, and private roads.

Association Fees

Monthly fees fund maintenance, services, and reserve accounts for future repairs.

Implications for Owners

Owners are responsible for everything within their unit walls and must carry appropriate insurance.
